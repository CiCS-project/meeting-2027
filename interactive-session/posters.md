# Sticker posters

Getting-to-know-each-other posters, based on Christian's hand-drawn sketch of 18 November 2026. Printable versions are in `posters.html`.

## Sticker rules

- **One sticker colour per institution:** TU/e, CWI, VU, UvA, RU, plus a sixth colour for other / guests. Each poster has an empty legend in its header: before hanging, put one sticker of each colour next to the institution's name.
- **One sticker per person per poster**, except:
  - Poster 1 (core challenges): **two** stickers per person.
  - Posters 5, 7 and 8 (tools, models, agents): **as many as apply**.
- Stickers aren't anonymous by name, but they are by colour. Speak to someone with a sticker near yours.

## The posters

| # | Question | Layout |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Which core challenges are closest to your work? | 3×3 grid: columns Crypto / HW / SW, holding the nine CiCS core challenges |
| 2 | Where did you go to primary school? | World map plus an enlarged map of Europe |
| 3 | Where did you get your PhD? | Same maps plus a "not yet / in progress" box |
| 4 | Where do you feel most at home? | Publication venues of CiCS papers so far (from CiCS-output, larger = more papers) plus empty boxes |
| 5 | Formal verification: which tools do you use? | Lean, EasyCrypt, Rocq (Coq), Isabelle, Jasmin, Formosa, other, unfamiliar |
| 6 | AI: how often do you use AI for research? | never → sometimes → always, one row per career stage (PhD student, postdoc, faculty, other) |
| 7 | AI: which models do you use? | Claude, ChatGPT, Mistral, open-weight, self-trained, other |
| 8 | AI agents: how do you run them? | YOLO, auto, manual approval, sandbox, I don't use agents |
| 9 | Are you worried about AI developments? | Quadrant: worry about job security (x) against worry about existential risk (y) |

## Notes

- The placement of the nine challenges in the Crypto / HW / SW columns follows the TU/e kick-off article as read in CiCS-output (`project/overview.md`). There, the third pillar is called "physical security" and the order is Crypto / SW / physical. Check with Tanja that the placement is right.
- Venues come from `publications/publications.json` in CiCS-output (79 papers). Preprint servers, datasets and reports are left out. The snippet to refresh them is in a comment at the top of `posters.html`.
- The maps need internet access when printing. They use public-domain Natural Earth outlines, loaded from jsdelivr with integrity hashes.
