# Interactive session: what are the most important problems for CiCS?

Material for the quadrant-voting session at the CiCS meeting of 27 January 2027, based on Marten van Dijk's proposal. Each poster states a question and a quadrant of two axes. Every participant places a sticky note with their name where they stand. The shape of the cloud of notes starts the discussion.

This folder is excluded from the website build (see `exclude` in `_config.yml`). The repository itself is public, so don't put anything confidential here.

| File | What |
|---|---|

## Timeline

| Time | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| Before 12:30 | Hang the posters along one wall, with a stack of sticky notes and pens under each one. Explain the voting briefly in the welcome. | Organisers |
| 14:30 – 14:50 | **Coffee break and voting.** Each person writes their name on one sticky note per poster and places it in the quadrant. | Everyone |
| 14:50 – 15:20 | **Plenary walk-along (about 5 min per poster).** For each poster, the facilitator describes the cloud: where is it dense, where are the outliers? Ask one or two people at the extremes to explain their position in a minute. | Facilitator per poster |
| 15:20 – 16:00 | **Small groups.** Pick the 4–6 posters with the most interesting clouds (consensus in the top-right, or a wide split). One group per poster of 5–8 people, mixed across institutes. Groups self-select, nudged towards mixing. Each group fills in `group-sheet.md`. | Groups, each with a volunteer note-taker |
| 16:00 – 16:20 | Coffee break and networking | |
| 16:20 – 16:50 | **Report back.** Each group gets 3–4 minutes: their conclusion, one concrete next step, and who wants to drive it. Then 5–10 minutes of plenary discussion on which next steps CiCS adopts. | Groups; chair: a PI |

The agreed next steps feed straight into the following items: "What do CiCS members need to be successful?", the RSP announcement, and the next meeting.

## Roles

- **Chair** (one PI): keeps time, runs the report-back and closes with the list of next steps.
- **Poster facilitators** (one per poster, ideally a mix of PIs and senior researchers): read the cloud aloud during the walk-along.
- **Note-takers** (one volunteer per group): fill in the group sheet and hand it to the organisers.
- **Photographer**: photographs every poster after voting, before anyone moves notes, for `results.md`.

## Materials checklist

- [ ] Posters printed (A2 or A1; A3 works for fewer than 25 participants), plus 2 blank quadrant posters for questions raised on the day
- [ ] Sticky notes, ~2 per participant per poster (about 400 for 30–35 people and 6 posters); a single colour is fine
- [ ] Felt-tip pens (thin markers are legible from a distance)
- [ ] Masking tape or poster putty; check with the VU room whether walls or boards may be used
- [ ] Printed group sheets (one per group plus spares)
- [ ] Timer or phone, and a bell for the group phase

## Rules for voting (read out in the welcome)

1. One note per person per poster, with your name on it. Votes aren't anonymous, because the point is to find each other.
2. You can skip a poster if you have no opinion.
3. You can add a short comment to your note (e.g. "only if we partner with a hospital").
4. Place your note during the first coffee break, before 14:50.
